WORKSPACE GUIDE

How to use DermaAudit AI

Follow these steps to prepare your image dataset, train a classifier, review predictions, and keep a clear research record.

01
Upload a labeled datasetStart from Datasets → Upload a dataset.

Upload one .zip file. Each image should be inside a folder named after its class.

my_dataset/
├── train/
│   ├── healthy/
│   │   └── image_001.jpg
│   └── affected/
│       └── image_002.jpg
└── test/
    ├── healthy/
    └── affected/

Remember: use at least two classes. JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, GIF, JFIF, TIF, and TIFF are supported.

02
Read the dataset profile and auditOpen the dataset after upload.

Check the detected class names, sample count, image dimensions, unreadable files, duplicates, class balance, and possible leakage. Fix warnings before training.

03
Train and compare modelsGo to Model training.

Select a ready dataset, choose an architecture, and start an experiment. Compare accuracy and Macro-F1 from the same dataset split. A completed run saves the checkpoint used for prediction.

04
Analyze a new imageOpen New prediction.

Upload a single image and review the predicted class, confidence percentage, and top alternative classes. Treat low-confidence results as a signal for expert review—not as a final diagnosis.

05
Review reports and manage historyUse Reports for the complete workspace view.

Reports shows dataset readiness, completed experiments, best accuracy, prediction count, and audit coverage. From Datasets, Experiments, Prediction history, or Reports you can clear old records; destructive cleanup always asks for confirmation.

Supported workflowDataset ZIP → Profile & audit → Train → New prediction → Reports
