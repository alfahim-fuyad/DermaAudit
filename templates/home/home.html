QUICK START GUIDE

Six steps to a trustworthy result

01
Upload your data

Bring a labeled ZIP, with class folders or a CSV/JSON manifest for flat images.

02
Validate the upload

Find unreadable files, missing labels, unsafe paths, unsupported formats, and empty classes before training.

03
Profile and audit

Review image statistics, class balance, exact and near duplicates, metadata, bias readiness, and leakage risk.

04
Prepare a controlled experiment

Use a 70/15/15 split, group-aware IDs when available, and choose an audit intervention without changing the original ZIP.

05
Train and compare

Compare held-out Macro-F1, balanced accuracy, per-class results, calibration, and early-stopping evidence.

06
Register, review, and monitor

Activate a checkpoint, inspect confidence and Grad-CAM, abstain when uncertain, and keep verified human review in the loop.

WORKSPACE GUIDE

How to use DermaAudit AI

Follow the evidence chain from dataset intake to monitored prediction. Each stage records what the system knows, what it cannot verify, and what still needs human review.

01
Upload a labeled datasetStart from Datasets → Upload a dataset.

Upload one .zip file. Each image should be inside a folder named after its class.

my_dataset/
├── train/
│   ├── healthy/
│   │   └── image_001.jpg
│   └── affected/
│       └── image_002.jpg
└── test/
    ├── healthy/
    └── affected/

Remember: use at least two classes. JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, GIF, JFIF, TIF, and TIFF are supported.

02
Validate, profile, and auditOpen the dataset evidence pipeline after upload.

Review supported formats, readable images, missing labels, dimensions, channels, metadata, exact and near duplicates, class balance, subgroup availability, and possible leakage. Blocking findings keep the dataset out of training.

03
Configure a controlled experimentGo to Model training.

Choose a ready dataset, architecture, split percentages, and intervention. Group IDs produce group-aware splits; otherwise the system uses deterministic class-aware stratification. Augmentation never enters validation or test data.

04
Evaluate and register evidenceOpen Reports and Experiments after training.

Review held-out accuracy, Macro-F1, balanced accuracy, precision, recall, AUROC, per-class metrics, confusion matrix, calibration, fairness availability, learning-rate history, and early stopping. Activate a completed checkpoint only after comparing the evidence.

05
Review and monitor predictionsOpen New prediction and Reports.

Upload one image to see the model class, calibrated confidence, top alternatives, and Grad-CAM when available. Low-confidence or close-margin results abstain and recommend qualified expert review. Reports tracks abstentions, queue size, class-mix drift, and the prerequisites for fairness drift.
